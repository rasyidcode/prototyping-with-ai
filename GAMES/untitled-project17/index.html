HOW TO PLAY

🧟 THE INVASION: Slay sways of zombies marching down the street towards your screen!

🖐️ SWIPE TO SLAP: Click-and-drag (Desktop) or Swipe (Mobile) over a zombie to whip your hand and fling them away in your swipe direction with speed-based force!

⚡ MEGA SHOCKWAVE: Hold the click still for 0.5s to charge up, then release to execute a massive shockwave pushing back all close zombies!

🛡️ HELMET ZOMBIES: Armored units that require 2 hits. The first slap staggers them and pops their helmet off!

☣️ HAZMAT ZOMBIES: Wearing bright orange, slapping them triggers a fiery blast clearing nearby zombies!

❄️ FROZEN ZOMBIES: Glowing cyan units that freeze time, slowing down all zombies for 4 seconds!

🔥 COMBOS: Slap in quick succession to stack multipliers for astronomical scores!

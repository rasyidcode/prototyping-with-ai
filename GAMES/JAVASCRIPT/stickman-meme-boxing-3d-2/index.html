HOW TO PLAY

W A S D

Circle around, dodge left/right, move in/out.

J

Quick punch. Low stamina cost. Fills Special meter.

K

Heavy punch. Higher stamina cost. Heavy damage.

Hold to block incoming punches. Watch your stamina!

L

Super Punch
Massive damage punch. Requires 100% Special Meter.

💡 Pro Boxing Tips

  • Don't spam punches! Exhausting your stamina leaves you staggered and unable to block.
  • Watch the opponent's arms. If they wind back, get ready to block or dodge!
  • Doge will automatically dodge a lot of your standing punches, time your hits after she misses!
  • John Cena will turn invisible, look for his glowing blue outline!
